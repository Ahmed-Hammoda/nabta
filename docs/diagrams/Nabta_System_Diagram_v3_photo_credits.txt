Notes
- The BME280 photo shows a SparkFun BME280 breakout, not the purple GY-BME280 board.
  It is the same sensor chip. Replace it with a photo of your own module if you can.
- CC BY and CC BY-SA photos need a credit line in the figure caption or the acknowledgements.
  CC BY-SA also asks that an adapted figure is shared under the same licence, which some
  journals do not accept. Your own photos of your own parts avoid this completely.
- In the PSD every picture is its own layer (group 04), named PHOTO ... or RENDER ...,
  so you can paste your own photo over it.
- In V3 the photos are in group 05 "Parts (photos)".
