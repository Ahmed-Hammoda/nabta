Build order

  1. Cut all pieces. Cut the window and the notch, drill the holes and vent slots before gluing.
  2. Glue base, outer walls and the two well walls. Seal the well's inside corners with silicone.
  3. Fix the Pi, breadboard, relay and DC jack. Wire them as on the wiring page.
  4. Build the stake: BH1750 on top of the head, BME280 in the side pocket, soil probe on the blade. Run the 5-core cable through the notch.
  5. Screw the post to the back wall, mount the arm and the light plate.
  6. Put the drip tray and spacers in the well, then the jar. Place the tank 50 from the right side and run the tube.
