Before you power on

  1. The 5 V 2.5 A adapter feeds only the pump and the light, through the relay's screw terminals. It never touches the Pi or the breadboard.
  2. Keep the JD-VCC jumper on the relay board. Most of these boards switch ON when IN is LOW; we set that in the code.
  3. Some breadboards split the side rails in the middle. If yours does, bridge each rail with a short wire.
  4. Water is measured by time. Run the pump into a measuring cup for 10 s, divide the mL by 10, and that is the pump's mL per second.
  5. For the real pot, move the BH1750 and BME280 next to the leaves on longer wires. The pins stay the same.
