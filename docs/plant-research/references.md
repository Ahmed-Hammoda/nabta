## Our own work (not from a source)

- The candidate scores (1 to 5) are the team's judgment based on the sources above.
- The lux to DLI conversion (1,000 lux ≈ 14 µmol/m²/s for white LEDs, ≈ 18.5 for sunlight) is a standard approximation, good for alerts only.
- The humidity bands and the moisture index thresholds are starting values, to be tuned in the calibration week (2 to 8 Nov).
