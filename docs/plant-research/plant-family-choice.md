# Plant family choice

**Owner:** Yassine Mtibaa · Week 1 task · 25 Oct 2026

## Recommendation

Grow **spearmint** (*Mentha spicata*, family Lamiaceae) in both pots and drop basil for this run.

Spearmint uses a lot of water, wilts visibly within a day or two of drying out, recovers fast, and tolerates cool, unheated rooms. Tunis outdoor lows are 11 °C in November and 8 °C in December [W2T]. Basil is in the same family but gets chilling damage below 12 °C, which is a real risk on December nights [HD]. Succulents and pothos use too little water to show any saving in four weeks, and cherry tomatoes need about 9 weeks to fruit plus strong grow lights [CG22].

## Selection criteria

Each candidate was scored from 1 (poor) to 5 (best) on six criteria taken from the study design: Pot A (Nabta) vs. Pot B (fixed schedule), indoors in Tunis, 9 Nov to 4 Dec 2026.

1. **Water use.** RQ1 measures water saved, so the plant must drink enough for a difference to show in 26 days.
2. **Fast, visible response.** Stress and recovery must show within days, so the sensors and the AI explanations have something to react to.
3. **Measurable stress.** Wilting, yellowing or growth loss that a camera, a photo log or a ruler can record.
4. **Fits the season indoors.** Tolerates Tunis winter light (about 10 h of daylight, 5 to 6 h of sun) and cool rooms.
5. **Easy and cheap to get in Tunis.** Two matching plants from one batch, replaceable if one dies.
6. **Local relevance.** A plant Tunisian households already grow and use, which helps the demo and the report.

## Candidate comparison

Spearmint scores highest (28 of 30). It is the only candidate that is thirsty, quick to react and comfortable in a cool Tunis winter room. The scores are our judgment based on the sources in the last column. Prices and availability have not been checked in shops yet.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Peppermint (*Mentha × piperita*) is a good substitute if spearmint is out of stock. It is more sensitive to drying than spearmint [FUM], so both pots must use the same species.

## Decision

Target family is **Lamiaceae** and target plant is **spearmint** (*na3na3*). Basil is dropped for the November to December run.

- **Why spearmint over basil:** same family and similar water use, but basil is damaged below 12 °C. Tunis averages an 8 °C low in December, and a pot near a window at night can get close to that. A cold-damaged basil would confuse the water comparison.
- **Why not the others:** succulents and pothos need water every one to three weeks, so Pot A and Pot B would barely differ. Tomatoes and chilis need more light than a Tunis window gives in winter, and more time than we have.
- **Backup plan:** if a pot dies, replace both with plants from the same batch and restart the count. Keep one spare spearmint pot from the start.
- **Basil later:** basil is a good second plant for a spring or summer study, when rooms stay above 18 °C.
