# Sensor stake v2 parts

New versions of three printed parts, received on 23 Nov 2026. The lantern and cap are now a larger hexagon.

| Part | v2 size (mm, outer box) | v1 size (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| `cap.step` | 90 × 78 × 13 | 46 × 40 × 9 |
| `lantern.step` | 88 × 76 × 58 | 44 × 38 × 37 |
| `housing.step` | 60 × 36 × 51 | 44 × 28 × 51 |

- `stem.step` and `window_disc.step` did not change in this update. Use the v1 files in [`../parts/`](../parts/) and check they still fit the new housing and lantern.
- There are no STL files for v2 yet. Export them from FreeCAD (**File → Export → STL**) before printing.
- `../build_stake.py`, the assembly files and `../print_stl/` are still the v1 design.
